Accessible composition

Build context around content—not over it

Long text wraps normally, native controls keep their semantics, and a single decorative layer stays behind the reading order.

Continue

Do

  • Use one ambient region for a hero or section.
  • Keep dense reading content on a solid Surface.
  • Verify light, dark, mobile and forced-colors behavior.

Don’t

  • Nest ambient backgrounds.
  • Place a separate animated field behind every card.
  • Treat decorative color as the only source of hierarchy.
