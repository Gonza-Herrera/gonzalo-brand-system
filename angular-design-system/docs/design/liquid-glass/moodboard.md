# Liquid Glass moodboard direction

This moodboard is a written direction, not a collection of interfaces to copy. Future exploration
should translate these qualities through the Gonzalo Herrera brand palette, type, spacing, content,
and engineering constraints.

## Material

- Frosted material with enough body to contain content.
- Smooth, stable surfaces rather than wet or distorted glass.
- Perceived thickness through coordinated edge, highlight, and shadow.
- Selective translucency that reveals atmosphere, not background detail.
- Solid reading areas used confidently beside translucent chrome.

Avoid crystal-clear panels, plastic shine, liquid distortion, heavy refraction, and transparent
containers that depend on a decorative backdrop to exist.

## Lighting

- Diffuse daylight in light theme.
- Restrained studio lighting in dark theme.
- One coherent light direction.
- Soft upper highlights and quiet lower separation.
- Ambient Lavender, Blue, or Peach appearing as environmental color.

Avoid neon rims, bright lens flares, metallic specular streaks, cursor spotlights, and several color
sources competing in one composition.

## Depth

- A calm background plane.
- One stable content plane.
- Occasional elevated or floating UI with a clear reason.
- Large areas separated by space before shadow.
- Compact temporary surfaces that feel close and precise.

Avoid deep stacks, card-on-card repetition, equal elevation for every object, and shadows used as
decoration.

## Composition

- Generous whitespace and clear reading paths.
- Large Manrope headings balanced by readable Inter body copy.
- A small number of material regions with deliberate alignment.
- Strong content blocks inside quieter surfaces.
- Ambient color placed at composition edges or behind a focal region.

Avoid dashboards filled with equally prominent tiles, overlapping panels without spatial meaning,
or effect-heavy backgrounds behind long-form content.

## Atmosphere

The intended atmosphere is:

- calm, not empty;
- premium, not ornamental;
- technical, not futuristic;
- human, not sterile;
- precise, not rigid;
- deep, not dark for its own sake;
- luminous, not glowing.

## Brand translation

| Brand quality         | Liquid Glass expression                                 |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Clarity               | Opaque content, restrained material, explicit hierarchy |
| Craftsmanship         | Consistent boundaries, radius logic, and fine lighting  |
| Leadership            | Calm composition and clear decision order               |
| Innovation            | Modern depth and translucency without spectacle         |
| Human warmth          | Ivory, Peach, Sand, and natural spacing                 |
| Engineering precision | Ink structure, Blue atmosphere, measured performance    |
| AI expression         | Lavender used as a controlled environmental accent      |

## Originality test

An exploration is on direction when it can be explained through brand principles and this material
model without naming another product. If recognition depends on reproducing another interface's
layout, control shape, reflection, or animation, the exploration is derivative and should be revised.

## Evaluation prompts

- Is the content still the first visual read?
- Does the material feel like it has body and a boundary?
- Is the atmosphere recognizably part of the existing palette?
- Can the same idea work in light and dark without inversion?
- Does the composition remain coherent with a Solid fallback?
- Is there one memorable brand decision rather than several borrowed effects?
